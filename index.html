How We Make It Happen

From the neighborhood lawn to your hands, our process is 100% sustainable, chemical-free, and community-driven.

1. Collection

Local high school volunteers rake and collect fallen leaves from community yards, preventing them from producing methane in landfills.

5M - 100 > 2M + 50
Explanation (M = Methane Prevented):
Subtract 2M: 3M - 100 > 50
Add 100: 3M > 150
Divide by 3: M > 50
We prevent over 50kg of methane gas emissions per neighborhood sweep!

2. Fiber Extraction

Using our eco-friendly mechanical process, we break down the dry leaves into strong, flexible fibers and natural bio-composites.

3C + 10 ≤ C + 10
Explanation (C = Chemicals Used):
Subtract C: 2C + 10 ≤ 10
Subtract 10: 2C ≤ 0
Divide by 2: C ≤ 0
We use absolutely zero harmful chemicals in our extraction process!

3. Manufacturing

The fibers are pressed and woven into highly durable, aesthetic paper products and reusable shopping bags.

4E - 20 ≤ E + 100
Explanation (E = Energy Used in kWh):
Subtract E: 3E - 20 ≤ 100
Add 20: 3E ≤ 120
Divide by 3: E ≤ 40
Our max energy expenditure per batch is capped at an ultra-low 40 kWh.

Obstacles We Have Overcome

Building a revolutionary sustainable company isn't easy. Here is the math behind some of the major hurdles we cleared in our first year of operations.

Weather Delays

Unpredictable rains heavily affected our collection schedule in the fall. We adapted by buying waterproof gear.

4R + 10 ≥ 2R + 30
R = Rainy Days:
4R - 2R ≥ 30 - 10 → 2R ≥ 20 → R ≥ 10
We survived over 10 consecutive rain-out days by shifting to indoor fiber processing.

Shipping Costs

Initial logistics costs threatened our margins. We solved this by partnering with local, carbon-neutral couriers.

5C - 500 ≤ 3C + 1500
C = Shipping Costs ($):
2C ≤ 2000 → C ≤ 1000
We successfully negotiated to keep monthly local shipping costs strictly under $1,000.

Prototype Failures

Our early leaf paper was too brittle. Our engineers went back to the drawing board repeatedly.

3P + 15 < P + 35
P = Prototype Failures:
2P < 20 → P < 10
We achieved structural integrity in under 10 iterations!

Funding the Dream

Industrial mechanical presses are expensive. We had to pitch to investors to afford our core machinery.

2F + 5000 ≥ 0.5F + 20000
F = Funding Raised ($):
1.5F ≥ 15000 → F ≥ 10000
Our community pitched in to help us secure the minimum $10,000 needed.

Delivery Logistics

Mapping out pickup routes for raw leaves took immense time. We optimized routes with custom software.

6D - 30 ≤ 2D + 50
D = Delay (Hours):
4D ≤ 80 → D ≤ 20
By utilizing our algorithm, total route delays are capped strictly under 20 hours a month.
