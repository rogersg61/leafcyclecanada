How We Make It Happen

From the neighborhood lawn to your hands, our process is 100% sustainable, chemical-free, and community-driven.

1. Collection

Local high school volunteers rake and collect fallen leaves from community yards, preventing them from producing methane in landfills.

2. Fiber Extraction

Using our eco-friendly mechanical process, we break down the dry leaves into strong, flexible fibers and natural bio-composites.

3. Manufacturing

The fibers are pressed and woven into highly durable, aesthetic paper products and reusable shopping bags.

Student Volunteer Program

Need community service hours? Want to make a real difference? Join the LeafCycle team! We offer a fun, outdoor experience that goes beyond just raking leaves.

  • Easy Volunteer Hours

    Earn verified community service hours simply by collecting and bagging fallen leaves in designated neighborhoods.

  • Built-in Biology Lessons

    Learn about local flora, the carbon cycle, and decomposition from our environmental scientists during collection drives.

  • $1,000 Scholarship Opportunity

    Every 10 hours volunteered enters you into our annual draw for a $1,000 college/university scholarship!

The Math Checks Out.

We believe in transparent, measurable impact. Here is how LeafCycle's environmental footprint compares to traditional manufacturing, expressed in simple inequalities.

  • 01. Trees_Cut(LeafCycle) = 0 < Trees_Cut(Std_Paper)
  • 02. Water_Usage(LeafCycle) < Water_Usage(Trad_Bags)
  • 03. CO2_Emissions(LeafCycle) < Plastic_CO2 * 0.1
  • 04. Decomp_Time(LeafCycle) < Decomp_Time(Plastic)
  • 05. Chemical_Bleach(LeafCycle) = 0 < Trad_Pulping
  • 06. Landfill_Leaf_Waste ↓ > Previous_Years
  • 07. Recyclability(LeafCycle) = 100% > Plastic(1%)
  • 08. Energy_Used_Per_Bag < Paper_Bag_Energy / 2
  • 09. Microplastics_Gen = 0 < Synthetic_Totes
  • 10. Biodiversity_Harm(LeafCycle) < Standard_Logging
  • 11. Student_Bio_Knowledge(Post) > Knowledge(Pre)
  • 12. Scholarships_Funded ≥ $1,000 / student
